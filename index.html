The Problem

Daily bus commuters face uncertainty about delays and overcrowding, especially during peak hours.

  • Commuters do not know whether the next bus will arrive on time or be delayed.
  • Popular routes become overcrowded, making boarding difficult and journeys uncomfortable.
  • Existing apps mainly show bus location or scheduled arrival time, not predicted crowd levels.
  • Passengers cannot compare buses based on both delay risk and expected occupancy.
  • Delays and crowding lead to longer waiting times, missed connections, and travel stress.
  • Transit operators lack simple insights into high-delay and high-crowding routes.

Our Solution

TransitPulse predicts both bus delay and passenger crowding before you travel.

Operator Dashboard

Why It Matters

TransitPulse turns transit data into practical decisions: commuters can avoid overcrowded buses, while operators can identify routes that need more capacity or better scheduling.

Tech Stack

A scalable architecture designed for a hackathon MVP and future real-world deployment.

Frontend

React / Next.js or Flutter for commuter and operator dashboards.

Backend

FastAPI or Node.js REST APIs for predictions, routes, and live data.

Machine Learning

Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, and time-series forecasting models.

Data

GTFS schedules, GPS feeds, historical arrivals, weather, and traffic data.

Database

PostgreSQL with PostGIS for routes and stops; Redis for live caching.

Deployment

Docker, cloud hosting, and scheduled model retraining pipelines.
